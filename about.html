Who we are

Started in a campus lab, not an office.

We are diploma classmates from vocational college. In 2024, the three of us started building fun practice websites together — simple pages, small experiments, and landing-page practice to sharpen our skills.

In 2025 we worked on our final year project as a team, learning how to deliver a complete website from start to finish. That project showed us we work best when we build together.

In March 2026 we joined internships, and by July 2026 we combined our experience into one team for this studio. Since then, we focus on landing pages, domain setup, hosting, and fast launch support.

We may still be early in our story, but we bring fresh skills, real teamwork, and the kind of care you get from people who built this from classwork to a studio.
